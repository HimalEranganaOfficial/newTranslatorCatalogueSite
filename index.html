Sworn translation checklist

Requirements can depend on the receiving authority, document type, court practice and current law. Confirm the applicable instructions with the relevant court, Ministry or receiving institution before relying on this guidance.

01

Multi-page integrity / corner seal

Where required by the relevant authority, ensure multi-page documents are securely linked and sealed across page edges to discourage page substitution. Confirm the exact method locally.

02

Language declaration

State the translation direction clearly at the top of the first page, for example, “Translation from Sinhala to English,” where appropriate.

03

End-of-translation marker

Use a clear ending marker when required by the recipient. Example: --- END OF TRANSLATION ---

04

Translator declaration

Include a declaration of accuracy and competence in a form accepted by the relevant authority. Avoid using a generic declaration without checking requirements.

05

Signature and official stamp

Confirm which signature, stamp or seal is required for the intended use. Wet-ink signatures and court stamps may be required in some workflows, but do not assume a single rule applies to every case.

06

Date clarification

Follow the receiving authority’s rules for execution dates. Do not omit a declaration date solely on the assumption that the source document already contains dates.

Important: This website is an independent directory interface, not an official government service. Listing data may be outdated or incomplete. For authoritative confirmation, contact the relevant Sri Lankan authority directly.
